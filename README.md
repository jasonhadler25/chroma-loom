# Chroma Loom

**Live interactive experience:** <https://jasonhadler25.github.io/chroma-loom/>

A zero-dependency, GPU-accelerated bioluminescent fluid & generative sound
instrument in a single HTML file:

- **Move or drag** (mouse or multi-touch) to weave real-time 2D Navier–Stokes
  fluid currents with vorticity confinement, bioluminescent bloom, and ACES
  filmic tonemapping.
- **Click / tap** anywhere to plant spinning gravity-vortex stars that stir
  spiral arms into the nebula and play harmonic chords (right-click to remove a
  star).
- **Five worlds & musical modes** (`1`–`5`): *Aurora* (F Lydian), *Coral* (Db
  Pentatonic), *Gold Nebula* (A Mixolydian), *Moonjelly* (Eb Hirajoshi), and
  *Ember* (C Dorian)—each driving a procedural Web Audio stereo shimmer-reverb
  synthesizer.
- **Shortcuts:** `K` (6-fold kaleidoscope), `A` (autonomous dream currents),
  `Space` (harmonic supernova), `C` (clear stars), `M` (mute/unmute), `S` (save
  PNG snapshot), `H` (hide UI).
